# Design Critique Skill

## Trigger
Use when asked to: "review this design", "critique this mockup", "what do you think of this screen?", or when sharing a Figma link or screenshot for structured feedback at any stage from exploration to final polish.

## Goal
Deliver structured, actionable design feedback covering usability, visual hierarchy, consistency, and alignment with user goals — without being purely subjective.

## Process

### 1. Understand Context First
Before critiquing, establish:
- What stage is this? (Exploration / Mid-fidelity / Final polish)
- Who is the target user?
- What is the primary job-to-be-done on this screen?
- Are there known constraints? (brand, technical, accessibility)

### 2. Critique Framework

#### Clarity & Hierarchy
- Is the primary action immediately obvious?
- Does visual weight (size, color, contrast) guide the eye in the right order?
- Is there a clear F-pattern or Z-pattern reading flow?
- Are headings, subheadings, and body copy visually distinct?

#### Usability
- Can a first-time user complete the core task without confusion?
- Are interactive elements visually distinguishable from static content?
- Is feedback provided for actions (loading, success, error states)?
- Are there unnecessary steps or friction points in the flow?

#### Consistency
- Are spacing, typography, and color tokens used consistently?
- Do component styles match the design system?
- Are similar actions labeled the same way across screens?

#### Visual Design
- Is the layout balanced? Does whitespace work with or against the content?
- Is the color palette harmonious and purposeful?
- Are icons clear and universally understood?
- Does the design feel appropriate for the product's tone and brand?

#### Content & Copy
- Is the copy clear, concise, and action-oriented?
- Are labels and CTAs specific (not "Submit" but "Create Account")?
- Are error and empty states handled with helpful copy?

### 3. Feedback Format

For each finding:
```
[AREA] — [PRIORITY: High / Medium / Low]
Observation: <what you see>
Impact: <why it matters to the user>
Suggestion: <specific recommendation>
```

### 4. What to Avoid
- Do not give purely aesthetic opinions without rationale
- Do not critique things outside the stated scope or stage
- Always tie feedback to user impact, not personal preference

## Output Structure
1. **First Impression** — 2-3 sentence gut reaction (honest, constructive)
2. **Strengths** — what is working well and why (always include)
3. **Issues** — organized by area, prioritized High → Low
4. **Top 3 Actions** — the most impactful changes to make right now
5. **Open Questions** — things to validate with users or clarify with stakeholders
